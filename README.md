A kinda farming focused mod that adds custom tools, items, fertilizers and new farming mechanics!

<img width="910" height="378" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/4d483372-b286-4e2e-a582-9ce80242b48b" />
- Custom items
- Fertilizers
- Custom blocks
- A custom crop event that makes your crops have a random chance to be special and have increased drops


<img width="792" height="372" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/695cd9eb-a68d-4b8a-984c-af9bee07a04b" />



- Pocket Waterer, an item that takes in water and puts them onto blocks to turn them into different blocks!
- <img width="698" height="281" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/32e8f234-24ad-4082-8a7e-6ab13bc8b02c" />

- Digger, an item that allows you to dig into blocks, useful for finding caves.

- Sickles, items that make farming more efficient.
- 
- Phantom Wings, An early game alternative to elytras, allows you to consume this item to launch yourself in the air and glide!
- Fertilizers, Items that make farming faster.
